Ng & Partners Law — Wellington
Sarah runs a boutique commercial practice in Wellington. The legal work is excellent. The intake and review process is quietly costing the firm thousands every week. Click each frustration to see the real number.
⏱
45–60 minutes per document review
Before every client call, Sarah or a senior associate reads the full contract — 30, 40, sometimes 80 pages — to identify the issues worth discussing. At $375/hr, that's $281–$375 of billable time spent on reading rather than advising.
⚠️
Risk clauses missed under time pressure
When a client needs turnaround in 24 hours, the review gets rushed. Personal guarantee clauses, early exit penalties, and rent review triggers — buried on page 31 — don't always surface in time. One missed clause can mean six-figure liability exposure.
🗂
New matters logged inconsistently
Each lawyer has their own intake process. Some log the matter immediately, some wait until after the first call. Client information sits in emails, sticky notes, and three different systems. Searching for context before a client call takes 15 minutes that shouldn't be necessary.
📅
Deadlines buried in documents — missed until urgent
Rent commencement dates, notice periods for lease options, consent deadlines — all buried in the document body. They surface when a client calls in a panic, or when the deadline has already passed. The NZ Property Council updated standard lease templates in early 2025 — new deadline structures that aren't always obvious at a glance.
